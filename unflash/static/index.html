Unflash

Detect and repair photosensitive-hazard flashing in videos.

  1. Open a video and Scan it — problem sections are found automatically (WCAG 2.x general-flash, red-flash and extended-flash analysis). You can also add sections by dragging on the timeline or typing timestamps.
  2. Open a section and Prepare it (proxy + thumbnails + per-frame analysis) — or use prepare all in the sidebar.
  3. Use Suggest (keep-light or keep-dark, optionally restricted to your selection) or mark frames yourself: removed frames are replaced by the last kept frame; extended frames linger for 1 s with silent audio.
  4. Check safety instantly, then Preview render to watch the result. If a check fails, select unsafe frames shows you exactly where the remaining problems are.
  5. Render full-res — this must be done for every section before exporting (render all in the sidebar does them all, skipping up-to-date ones). Sections whose edits changed after rendering are flagged render stale; re-render them.
  6. Export stitches the rendered sections back into the untouched video, then Verify re-scans the final file.

This tool reduces risk; it cannot guarantee safety. It does not detect hazardous static patterns (e.g. fine stripes), and individual sensitivity varies — review flagged sections yourself before sharing.
